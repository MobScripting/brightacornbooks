Ash and Bloom

Book One of The Tended Earth

The sky is changing. The forest is listening.

Something fell into the Blue Ridge mountains — a seed, gestating, quietly remaking the world in its own image. The trees are wrong. The animals are wrong. And the Song it sings is getting louder.

Mara Quinn, 34, ex-hotshot firefighter turned homesteader, wants nothing to do with it. But the fire that took her crew taught her one thing the Veyr never counted on: fire is its great weakness. With her dog Cinder at her side, Mara is about to learn what it costs to burn a world — and what it means to be tuned to its Song.

About the author

S. R. Ashford writes science fiction about survival, fire, and the strange kindness of dangerous places. Ash and Bloom, the first book of The Tended Earth, began as a story about the Blue Ridge mountains and the people stubborn enough to live in them.
